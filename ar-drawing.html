Adjust your tracing image

Drag the photo with one finger. Pinch with two fingers to resize. Lock it when lined up with the paper.

How to draw

1. Upload a photo from your gallery.

2. Allow camera access and point the rear camera at paper on a flat surface.

3. Make the picture faint, drag and resize it to fit, then lock it so it will not move accidentally.

4. Keep your phone steady on a stand above the paper and follow the lines while watching the screen.

Note: The image appears on your phone screen, not projected onto the paper. The phone should remain still for accurate tracing.

🔒 Photos are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to a server.
